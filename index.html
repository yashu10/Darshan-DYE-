Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what buyers ask us most.

Are you a manufacturer or a trader?

Darshan Dye-Chem is a manufacturer, and also supplies wholesale and retail. We are based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Which dyes do you make?

Our range includes acid dyes, direct dyes and other specialty colourants. You can browse every shade, with its ratings, in the shade explorer and download our shade cards as PDFs.

Can I get a sample before ordering in bulk?

Yes. Request a sample through the contact page with the dye and shade you need, and our team will confirm the details.

Which dye is right for my material?

Acid dyes are commonly used for wool, silk, nylon and leather, and direct dyes for cotton, other cellulosic fibres and paper. Try our dye finder or ask our team.

Do you supply overseas buyers?

Yes, export enquiries are welcome. Share your product, quantity and destination country and we will respond with options.

How do I get pricing?

Send us your requirement through the contact page, WhatsApp or phone with the dye name, quantity and delivery location, and we will reply with a quote.

Need a dye for your process?

Share the shade, quantity and destination. We will come back with pricing and sample options.
