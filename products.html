Acid vs direct dyes

Which dye family fits your material?

Acid dyes are commonly used on wool, silk, nylon and leather. Direct dyes are commonly used on cotton, other cellulosic fibres and paper.

Not sure which to choose? Tell us your material and process and our team will advise.
